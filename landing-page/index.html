Diamond Hands Protocol

A permissionless vault factory on Base Network where every deposit and withdrawal pays a tax that flows to holders as dividends and burns tokens forever. The longer you hold, the more you earn from those who don't.

Permissionless

Anyone can deploy a vault for any ERC-20 token. No permissions required.

Dividend Distribution

Every tax event distributes dividends proportional to holdings. Holders earn from every transaction.

Protocol Fee

0.5% of every tax goes to the protocol treasury. Community-owned and transparent.

How It Works

Deposit & Earn

Users deposit tokens into the vault. A portion of every deposit is taxed.

Tax Distribution

Every tax event splits: dividends to holders, 0.5% protocol fee, remainder burned.

Claim Dividends

Users can claim their accumulated dividends at any time. Paid in the underlying token.
